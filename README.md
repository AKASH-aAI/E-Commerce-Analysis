# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to uncover valuable business insights using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

The objective of this project was to perform end-to-end data analysis, starting from data cleaning and exploratory data analysis to building an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses generate massive amounts of transactional data every day. However, raw data alone does not provide meaningful insights for business growth and strategic decision-making.

The goal of this project was to:

- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze product category performance
- Identify preferred payment methods
- Study customer demographics
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights

# 📊 Key Insights

### Sales Performance

The business generated approximately **83.47M** in total sales from more than **66K orders**, indicating strong transaction activity.

### Product Category Analysis

Books and Clothing categories generated the highest revenue, while Electronics contributed comparatively lower sales.

### Customer Demographics

Sales were distributed almost equally across Male, Female, and Other customer groups, indicating a balanced customer base.

### Age Group Analysis

Customers aged **55+** generated the highest sales volume, making them the most valuable customer segment.

### Payment Method Analysis

Bank Transfer and PayPal emerged as the most preferred payment methods among customers.

### Monthly Sales Trend

Sales remained relatively stable throughout the year with minor fluctuations, indicating consistent customer demand and purchasing behavior.
